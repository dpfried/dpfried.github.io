Prospective graduate students

Am I taking students? I'll be recruiting a small number of graduate students this admissions cycle, to start working together in Fall 2027. I'm particularly interested in working on interactive systems, AI agents, communication in multi-agent systems, and pragmatics.

How to apply. The easiest way to work with our group (and many great folks in LTI) is to apply to LTI's graduate programs (PhD, MLT, and professional master's programs). The application deadline for this year is December 9th, 2026, 3pm EST (there is also an early deadline of November 18th, 2026, with a reduced application fee). There's more information here. For questions about the application process, please check these pages: instructions, FAQ.

Should you email me? Emailing me won't affect your chances of admission. I get too many emails unfortunately and generally can't reply to ones about admissions. Sorry about that! I really appreciate your interest and time applying.
