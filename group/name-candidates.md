# Lab name candidates

Running list of names considered for the group page. The site currently uses
PAIR as a placeholder. Criteria that came up: reads as a real word, short
(4–5 letters), and distinctive enough that another LTI lab couldn't plausibly
claim it (which usually means a letter for Pragmatics or Norms). Should say
something about agents.

## Promising

- **GRIP** — Grounding, Reasoning, Interaction, Pragmatics. Four letters, real
  word, the P makes it distinctive. Liked.
- **GRICE** — after Paul Grice. Tried "Grounded Reasoning and Interaction ·
  Communicating Efficiently". Set aside because there's no letter for agents;
  options that fix it: "…Communicating Efficiently with Agents",
  "Grounded Reasoning, Interaction, and Communication for Efficient Agents",
  "Grounded Reasoning and Interaction for Agents that Communicate Efficiently".
  Written GRIC it gets read as "grick"/"Greek", so keep the E.
- **SIGIL** — Situated Interaction, Grounding, Inference (or Intent), Language.
  Five letters; a sigil is a sign that carries meaning. Slight occult flavor;
  collides with an ebook editor and a game.
- **LAIR** — preferred expansion: **Language Agents, Interaction, Reasoning**
  ("language agents" as a unit). Playful, short. Other L words considered:
  Learning, Listeners (speaker-listener nod).
- **LEAP** — Language, Efficiency, Agents, Pragmatics. Four letters; has both
  agents and pragmatics.
- **LINK** — Language, Interaction, Norms, Knowledge. "Link" reads as grounding;
  Norms keeps it distinctive.
- **GRAIL** — Grounded Reasoning, Agents, Interaction, and Language. Liked the
  vibe; somewhat generic.
- **SIGNAL** — Situated Interaction, Grounding, Norms, Agents, Language. Liked
  the expansion, but six letters is too many.
- **CORAL** — Communication, Reasoning, Agents, Language. Liked; short and
  clean. Concern: generic enough that any LTI lab could claim it.

## Considered

- **PARLEY** — negotiation between parties; loose acronym. We don't do much
  negotiation.
- **PLAN** — Pragmatics, Language, Agents, Norms. Common word; may collide.
- **GRIN** — Grounding, Reasoning, Interaction, Norms.
- **GRAIN** — Grounding, Reasoning, Agents, Interaction, Norms.
- **SIGN** — Situated Interaction, Grounding, Norms. A bit flat.
- **TACIT** — Theory of mind, Agents, Communication, Interaction, Teaching.
  Forced expansion.
- **CAIRN** — Collaborative Agents, Interaction, Reasoning, Natural language.
- **LARK** — Language, Agents, Reasoning, Knowledge. Lighter sibling of LAIR.
- **LENS** — Language, Environments, Norms, Situated. S is a stretch.
- **PILLAR** — Pragmatics, Interaction, Language, Learning, Agents, Reasoning.
  Too long.
- **TRAIL** — Trained Reasoning Agents that Interact and Learn. Forced.
- **LIRA** — Language, Interaction, Reasoning, Agents. Currency; noisy search.
- **PRAGMA**, **CLAIR**, **INTERACT**, **Common Ground** — early suggestions.

## Ruled out

- **PAIR** (current placeholder) — collides with Google's People + AI Research.
- **GLINT** — Grounded Language and Interaction; taken by Jenn Hu, and we do
  more than grounding now.
